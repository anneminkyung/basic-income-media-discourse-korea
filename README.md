# Media Discourse on UBI-Related Cash Transfer Policies in South Korea (2022-2025)

## Overview

This repository contains code and analysis for a research project examining the evolution of South Korean media discourse on Universal Basic Income (UBI)–related and quasi-UBI cash transfer policies between 2022 and 2025. Leveraging a mixed-method approach that combines topic modeling, sentiment analysis, and temporal statistical tests, the study analyzes 8,930 news articles collected from the BigKinds database.

The project focuses on how media framing and sentiment surrounding UBI-like policies change over time, particularly across presidential election periods and phases of active policy implementation. It highlights a dual dynamic in Korean media discourse, where UBI-related policies function simultaneously as highly contested electoral issues and as increasingly institutionalized policy instruments through **UBI policy experimentation**.

## Research Questions

* How does media sentiment toward UBI-related cash transfer policies in South Korea evolve over time?
* How do presidential election periods affect the framing, sentiment, and volatility of UBI-related media discourse?
* What thematic shifts occur as UBI-like policies move from politically contested proposals to concrete policy experimentation and implementation?
